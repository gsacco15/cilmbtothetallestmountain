Staying alive

  • Follow the orange flags. The gold light marks the summit.
  • Eat red berries and brown mushrooms. Red mushrooms with white spots make you sick.
  • Drink from lakes and ponds. Up in the snow, scoop snow for water, but it makes you cold.
  • Campfires keep you warm and animals stay away from camps.
  • Open the supply crate at each camp once a day. Sleep in a tent to skip the night.
  • Up high the air is thin, so you get tired faster.
  • Walk into Rielle's purple boxes to get random stuff: floor cleaner, a stethoscope, a rubber chicken, roller skates and more. Try them all.
